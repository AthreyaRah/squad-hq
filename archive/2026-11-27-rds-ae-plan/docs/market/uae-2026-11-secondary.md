## Requirements in sampled AE postings (Dubai, 2026)

- Years: 3+ for mid-level (Stake, Chalhoub, talabat AE II); 5+ for senior (Cognita, others).
- dbt: the most consistent requirement (talabat), or strongly preferred (Built In listing).
- SQL advanced; Python commonly required.
- Dimensional modeling (Kimball, star schema): recurring.
- Databricks (Unity Catalog, Delta, Lakeflow): some roles (Cognita); others accept BigQuery or Snowflake.
- Orchestration (Airflow, Dagster, dbt Cloud): several.
