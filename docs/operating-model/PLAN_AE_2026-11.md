# PLAN: Analytics Engineer / BI Engineer track (from 2026-11-27)

This plan replaces the slice **order** in CLAUDE.md for the first job move. The slices, the ticket model and the teaching rules still hold. Evidence is in `docs/market/uae-2026-11-secondary.md`; TKT-030 adds the primary postings.

## 1. Goal

A UAE **Analytics Engineer** or **BI Engineer** role at **≥30k AED/month**. Interviews running before Ramadan (about 2027-02-08). Offer by late Feb–Mar 2027.
- **Safety net, applied to in parallel:** Senior Data Analyst / Senior BI Analyst roles at top-paying employers. These reach 30–35k only at the top of their 20–35k band.
- **Move 2 (after the switch):** Data Engineer / data intelligence engineer. This is where S3–S6 come back.

## 6. Risks I see now

1. **Years of experience.** AE postings gate on 3+ years. You present the real number. If it is short, banks and vendors weigh certs and projects more than e-commerce does.
2. **The Genie medallion was written by AI.** It is interview risk until AE2 makes it yours.
3. **Python is L1.** AE1 fixes the basics; the Python interview round stays the weakest round until about mid-Jan.
4. **Arabic and Emiratisation** shrink the government pool. Apply through vendors as well.
